Questions

Before you request.

What if no van is near me?

Dispatch keeps searching and escalates to a human dispatcher. If we cannot reach you within the promised window, you can cancel at no cost.

Do I need to stay with the car?

We recommend it. If you need to step away, confirm with the technician first — they will call when they arrive and walk you through the handover.

Which connectors do you support?

CCS2, CHAdeMO, and Type 2 in the demo. Connector compatibility is checked before a van is assigned, so you never wait for the wrong van.

Is this a real service?

This is a design concept for an internship project. The fleet is simulated, the stats are targets, and payments use Razorpay test mode.

Direction A · design notes

Calm Confidence

A service brand that happens to be technology. Lower the heart rate before asking for a decision.

Compare directions

Why this direction

The reasoning behind the surface

A stranded driver is not browsing. They are anxious, possibly on a hard shoulder, and need one obvious next action. This direction treats the product like a calm service counter: soft surfaces, one primary CTA, an ETA that never disappears, and language that explains rather than sells.

  • The ETA ring and status rail make waiting feel supervised, not vague.
  • Sage green and generous whitespace lower urgency without hiding the action.
  • Cards map cleanly to reusable blocks: ETA card, driver card, timeline, queue row.
  • The light palette demos well on a projector and keeps text contrast comfortable.

What to watch

Where the direction could fail

  • Light rounded cards can drift into generic SaaS without a strong wordmark or photography.
  • The airy card system is too slow for a dense dispatch board — ops needs a tighter variant.
  • Status relies on green/amber; every state also needs a text label and icon.
  • Too many soft surfaces can feel bland. Add one confident ink panel for contrast.

Choose this if

You want the customer experience to feel trustworthy and human, and you are willing to give the dispatch console a denser sub-theme of its own.

Main risk

It is the safest and least distinctive of the three. The wordmark, illustration, and photography have to carry more personality than the layout does.
